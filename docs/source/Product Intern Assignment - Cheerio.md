## **Assignment A \- Make customer support scale for non-technical users**

**Problem:** Our support team cannot keep up as users grow. Most users are non-technical. They get stuck on setup (WhatsApp API, templates, chatbot flows, integrations) and raise tickets for things they could do themselves. More users means more tickets and more support hires.

**Your task:** Design and prototype a solution that resolves most of these queries without a human, involving human wherever required. Explore the different ways this could be solved, prioritise them, and pick one.

### **Part 1 \- Solution (approx. 2-3 pages)**

1. Who is the user and what are the top 5 queries or issues you would solve first? Why these?  
2. List all the possible solutions you can think of. Prioritise them and explain why you picked your final one.  
3. Describe your chosen solution. What will it do, and what will it not do?  
4. When does a human still need to step in?  
5. How will you measure success? Pick 3 metrics.  
6. Define a North Star metric: Choose the single metric that best captures the value your solution delivers. Set a realistic target for improving it over the next 3 months (e.g., improve it by X%) and explain how you would achieve that improvement.

### **Part 2 \- Design prototype**

1. Create a clickable design prototype of your chosen solution (AI design tools like v0, Lovable, or Claude). No need to build a working product.  
2. Cover at least 1 real user scenario end to end, including the key screens and edge cases.  
3. Share the link and 3-4 screenshots.

### **Part 3 \- How you used AI**

1. Which AI tools did you use and for what?  
2. Share 3 prompts that worked well and 1 that failed. What did you change?  
3. Where did AI give a wrong or weak answer, and how did you catch it?  
4. Which parts did you do yourself, without AI?

### **Part 4 \- Numbers: cost, billing and audit**

Do this in a Google Sheet. Maintain your formulas and show assumptions clearly.

1. **Guesstimate:** Cheerio has 5,000 active businesses. Estimate the monthly support queries and how many your solution will resolve.  
2. **Running cost:** Estimate the cost per query and per month. If your solution uses AI, include token cost (assume a model price per 1M input and output tokens and state your source).  
3. **Billing:** How should we charge for this? Free, per conversation, credits or a plan add-on? Suggest a price and show the margin.  
4. **Audit:** How will you audit usage and billing every month to make sure we stay profitable? What alerts or checks will you set up?  
5. **Savings:** Compare your solution's cost with the cost of human support for the same volume.
